# Proposed OpenKline Source-Available Community License v0.1

Status: **Owner-confirmed policy draft; pending qualified legal-counsel review,
copyright-holder adoption, and ownership/provenance confirmation. Not an
effective license grant.** This document does not grant a license, alter a root
`LICENSE`, change npm metadata, authorize redistribution, or authorize any
publication.

This is a custom source-available license proposal. It is not approved by the
Open Source Initiative (OSI), must not be called an OSI-approved license, and
is not assigned an SPDX identifier by this project.

the Consumer Application using the Work satisfies every condition in section 3.

the authorized OpenKline copyright holder.

### 2. Definitions

- **Consumer Application** means software, a website, a hosted service, a
  mobile or desktop application, a plugin, or a distributed product that makes
  functionality using the Work available to an end user. It includes an
  application that uses the Work in a browser, on a server, in a build, or
  through a service API for the benefit of end users.
- **Free Application** means a Consumer Application whose complete required
  user-facing functionality is available to every end user without monetary
  charge or other consideration. A fee, purchase, subscription, token, paid
  account, paid usage allowance, or other payment required to access, retain,
  accelerate, unlock, or improve functionality means the application is not
  free for this license.
- **Publicly Available Source Code** means the complete source code reasonably
  necessary to build, run, modify, and deploy the Consumer Application's
  required functionality, made available to the public without payment, NDA,
  approval gate, or discriminatory access restriction. It includes the
  application code, build/deployment instructions, configuration templates,
  and the source of required service components. Secrets, private credentials,
  and personal data may be excluded only if documented replacement values and
  reproducible instructions are supplied. The public source must correspond to
  the version made available to end users no later than that availability.
- **Source-Available** describes this policy's public-source condition. It does
  not itself mean that the Work or Consumer Application is "Open Source" under
  the OSI Open Source Definition. Any compatibility limits must be disclosed.
- **Voluntary Donation** means money or property voluntarily given without a
  requirement to use the Consumer Application and without any product benefit,
  including no feature, privileged access, faster service, higher limit,
  priority support, badge, status, vote, or other advantage.
- **Advertising** means displaying, serving, embedding, targeting, or accepting
  consideration for promotional material; it includes paid placements,
  sponsored content, revenue-generating affiliate links, referral promotions,
  and equivalent arrangements. Neutral attribution required by an applicable
  license is not Advertising solely because it identifies a rightsholder.
- **Paid Feature** means any functionality, content, integration, capacity,
  priority, support entitlement, access tier, or product benefit offered in
  exchange for money or other consideration.
- **Subscription Access** means recurring or time-limited payment, membership,
  credit, token, or account plan required for or tied to using any Consumer
  Application functionality, including usage limits or priority capacity.
- **Redistribution** means providing, conveying, publishing, selling, giving,
  hosting for download, embedding in a distributable product, or otherwise
  making the Work or a modified Work available to another person or entity.
- **Hosted Application / SaaS** means a Consumer Application whose functionality
  is made available over a network, whether or not users receive a copy of its
  software. Hosted use is use of the Work for purposes of this proposed license.
- **Modification** means a change to the Work. **Derivative Work** has the
  meaning required by applicable copyright law; this proposed license does not
  resolve whether a particular link, API use, deployment, or combination is a

### 3. Conditional permission

Subject to all terms of this proposed license, the copyright holder would grant
You a non-exclusive, worldwide, no-charge permission to use, copy, modify, and
Redistribute the Work only in connection with a Consumer Application that
satisfies **all** of the following conditions:

1. It is a Free Application for all end users.
2. Its required source code is Publicly Available Source Code.
3. Its source availability is genuine rather than nominal, and its repository,
   documentation, or user-facing licensing materials clearly disclose this
   Work's source-available terms and any compatibility limitations.
4. It offers no Paid Features, premium tiers, Subscription Access, paid access,
   or paid product benefits.
5. It displays no Advertising, paid promotional placement, or revenue-
   generating affiliate promotion.
6. It may solicit or receive only Voluntary Donations. Donations must not
   provide features, privileged access, higher usage limits, priority, or any
   other Consumer Application benefit.
7. A company or organization is not disqualified solely because it earns
   unrelated revenue. Revenue tied to the Consumer Application must still
   satisfy every condition above.

### 4. Redistribution, modifications, and notices

You may Redistribute the unmodified or modified Work only if the recipient
receives this proposed license (or, after adoption, the final applicable
license), all applicable copyright and attribution notices, and clear notice of
your modifications. A Modification or Derivative Work remains subject to the
conditions of section 3 when used in or offered through a Consumer Application.
You must make the source of distributed Modifications publicly available on the
same conditions and must not present modified or redistributed OpenKline
components as exempt from the applicable license obligations.

You may add notices for your own work, but may not remove or misrepresent
OpenKline copyright, license, or attribution notices. Required third-party
notices remain separate obligations and are not replaced by this proposed
license.

### 5. Hosted applications

A Hosted Application / SaaS using the Work is a Consumer Application and must
meet section 3. In particular, making only browser code public is insufficient
if non-public server code is required to provide the functionality offered to
end users. Running the Work on a server does not avoid the Free Application,
Publicly Available Source Code, no-Advertising, no-Paid-Feature, or donation
conditions.

### 6. Commercial exceptions and reserved rights

Any use outside section 3, including a closed-source, paid, subscription,
advertising-supported, affiliate-supported, premium, or otherwise nonconforming
Consumer Application, requires a separate written license from the authorized
OpenKline copyright holder. No such commercial exception is implied, offered,
or approved by this proposal.

Except for the conditional permission above, all rights are reserved. This
proposal grants no trademark right and makes no express patent grant. Counsel
and the copyright holder must decide whether the final adopted license should
include patent language.

The conditional permission ends automatically if You do not meet its
conditions. A separate written license may state a cure or reinstatement right.
The Work would be provided "AS IS" without warranty to the maximum extent
permitted by law, and the final text should contain counsel-approved limitation

### 8. Adoption and interpretation

This proposed text has no legal effect until the authorized copyright holder
adopts counsel-reviewed final text in an effective distribution. Before that
time, no person may rely on it as a license grant. Questions about scope,
derivative works, jurisdiction, enforceability, patents, or compatibility must
be resolved by qualified counsel and, where needed, a separate written license.

## Policy summary

The policy permits qualifying free, publicly source-available consumer
applications, including those operated by companies with unrelated revenue. It
does not permit monetizing the Consumer Application with paid features,
subscriptions, advertising, paid promotions, or revenue-generating affiliates.
Voluntary donations are allowed only when they give donors no product benefit.
See [examples](./licensing-policy-examples.md) and the
[compatibility analysis](./license-compatibility-analysis.md).
